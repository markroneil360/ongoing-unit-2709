Verified outside-guide exceedance intervals, each shorter than 30 minutes.

Tier 3
Prolonged exceedance

One or more continuous verified exceedances lasting 30–119 minutes.

One or more continuous verified exceedances lasting 120 minutes or longer.

Sources and methods

How to read this page

  • HDF is the air-pressure/infrasound channel. EHZ is vertical ground motion. Their amplitudes are never combined.
  • The public detector finds timing and frequency from one-second instrument records. Its detector-window length is not time above an outside guide.
  • A nominal HDF pressure estimate may be shown using 56,000 counts/Pa (manufacturer estimate, ±10%). Standards grading waits for the response, weighting, bands, location and averaging period each guide requires.
  • Percent above/below will appear only when a calibrated R6E8A pressure and a named guide use matching units. Decibel values are never divided to create a percentage.
  • Missing samples stay missing. No interpolation, zero-filling, source identification, medical conclusion or danger label is added.
*This Data may lag. For personal verification, data can be found through the Raspberry Shake FDSN service and the channel viewers above. This dashboard documents instrument records and published-guide readiness; it does not identify a source, cause, person, medical effect or safety condition.
