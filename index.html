What management should notice

Why these five are abnormal

  • Every listed event persisted for more than 30 minutes.
  • Three events overlapped the 10 PM–7 AM quiet-hours review window.
  • The overnight events were not single spikes; they were sustained pressure-channel abnormalities.
  • Dominant frequencies are shown exactly as measured in the event summaries; frequencies outside 4–8 Hz are not relabeled as 4–8 Hz.
  • HDF pressure and EHZ vertical motion remain separate physical measurements throughout.
Key interpretation boundary

Abnormal does not automatically mean medically dangerous

The dashboard deliberately separates instrument abnormality from a medical conclusion. Duration, recurrence, quiet-hour timing, calibrated level and frequency all matter. Source identity and health causation require additional evidence.

Published benchmark stack

External standards used as screens, not invented limits

1 · ISO 7196

G-weighted infrasound method

1–20 Hz

Defines the G-weighting characteristic for infrasound measurement. It is a measurement method, not an allowable-hours dose.

ISO 7196 source
2 · Danish EPA

Indoor infrasound screen

85 dB(G)

Recommended G-weighted infrasound level for occupied residential-type rooms, averaged indoors over 10 minutes. Used here as an external screening comparator, not Michigan law.

Danish EPA source
3 · WHO

Nighttime sleep context

<30 dB(A)

WHO community-noise guidance for bedrooms at night. This is A-weighted sleep context and is not converted into a 4–8 Hz pressure threshold.

WHO source
4 · ASHRAE / ANSI

Hotel-room background context

NC/RC

Hotel HVAC/background comfort context. These criteria are kept separate from 4–8 Hz infrasound screening and from building-assembly STC/IIC ratings.

ASHRAE source
Health-evidence wording

Why the dashboard avoids unsupported certainty

Very high infrasound levels can produce acute effects, but authoritative reviews have not established that ordinary environmental exposure at a particular single frequency—such as 7 Hz—automatically causes injury. The dashboard therefore reports measured pressure, sustained duration, recurrence and benchmark comparisons, while leaving medical causation to clinical evaluation and calibrated field investigation.

Public-use note

How this resource should be read

This page is designed to help building management quickly identify prolonged abnormal pressure events, especially during expected quiet hours, and then verify the underlying data through Raspberry Shake. It documents the instrument record; it does not identify a person, device, building system, intent, or medical cause.
