What can be compared now

N/A LEVEL

ISO 7196 - infrasound

HDF event frequencies can fall within the approximate 0.25-20 Hz scope. A numeric dB(G) comparison requires calibrated pressure and G-weighting, which the current raw-count feed does not provide.

N/A LEVEL

ANSI/ASA S12.2 and ASHRAE RC/NC

Guest-room RC/NC assessment requires calibrated octave-band room sound-pressure levels. HDF counts and EHZ motion are not converted into an NC/RC grade.

N/A LEVEL

Defra NANR45 - 10-160 Hz

A direct comparison requires calibrated 1/3-octave sound-pressure values. The available raw-count summary is not substituted for those values.

N/A LEVEL

WHO night noise and ISO 1996

These environmental acoustic methods require their specified sound metrics and averaging periods. EHZ remains seismic context only and is never assessed against hotel acoustic criteria.

Sources and methods

Verification and interpretation controls

  • Live waveform display: official Raspberry Shake DataView embeds for R6E8A HDF and EHZ.
  • Derived event status: Raspberry Shake FDSN DataSelect, requested in windows no longer than 24 hours.
  • HDF pressure/infrasound and EHZ vertical motion are processed, labeled and reported independently.
  • Raw MiniSEED is not redistributed by this dashboard. Derived raw-count summaries are not dB(A), dB(C), dB(G), NC or RC.
  • External references are named only for the metric they govern. When calibration, weighting, frequency bands or coverage are insufficient, the result is N/A.
  • Missing samples are not interpolated, zero-filled or counted as event time.
*This Data may lag. For personal verification, data can be found at Raspberry Shake FDSN and in the official DataView panels above. HDF and EHZ are independent instrument records; this dashboard does not identify a source, cause or person.
