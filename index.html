What the percentages mean

  1. HDF pressure is evaluated first; EHZ remains an independent vertical-motion record.
  2. 100% equals the named guide for threshold panels. The headline ISO percentage is an energy-share measure inside its method band.
  3. Tier 2 follows the supported ASHRAE bands after the manufacturer sensitivity tolerance is applied.
  4. Every percentage uses only its named external published guide.

*This data may lag. For personal verification, source data can be found through Raspberry Shake FDSN. The displayed percentages index the supplied instrument record against published guidance; a complete room evaluation adds complementary full-band instrumentation and site context.
