Interpretation boundary

What this instrument record establishes

The record establishes measured pressure behavior, frequency content, duration, recurrence, timing, calibrated low-frequency levels where response correction was completed, and exact coverage. It does not by itself identify a source, intent, or medical causation. Those questions require separate evidence.
